About the yard

Thirty years of sawdust

Liemex, trading as Baltic Pine & European Timber, imports and produces solid timber fixtures and fittings from a yard and machine shop in Port Adelaide.

We specialise in timber that is meant to last: floors, doors, mouldings and joinery timber, produced by experienced machinists and sold with straight advice.

The range

Hardwood flooring, decking, cladding, lining, benchtops and custom fixtures, 356 lines in all. It suits a villa restoration as well as a commercial fit-out, and most of it can be machined to another size or species.

Who we supply

Resellers, builders and joiners have bought from us for three decades, from standard profiles to one-off runs. Homeowners are just as welcome: bring your plans and we'll go through them with you.

Sawn, dressed, profiled, finished

The traditional 152 × 22 Baltic pine floorboard, end on. We saw, dress and profile hardwood and softwood on our own moulders, laminate benchtops and posts, and lacquer profiles to an architect's specification.
