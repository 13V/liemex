Timber species

In the rack

Grain and colour change from board to board, so use these as a guide, then come and see the boards in daylight.

  • 01 · Hardwood

    Tasmanian oak

    Elegant and durable, at home in both modern and classic interiors.

    Flooring · Skirting · Architraves

  • 02 · Softwood

    Baltic pine

    Light-coloured softwood with a smooth finish. The traditional Adelaide floorboard.

    Flooring · Lining · Joinery

  • 03 · Hardwood

    Spotted gum

    High-strength timber with lively natural colour variation.

    Decking · Flooring · Structural

  • 04 · Hardwood

    Jarrah

    Dense West Australian hardwood in deep reds, supplied as flooring, decking and stair treads.

    Flooring · Decking · Stairs

  • 05 · Hardwood

    Victorian ash

    Clean, consistent appearance and a fine grain. Machines and finishes well.

    Flooring · Stairs · Joinery

  • 06 · Hardwood

    Australian chestnut

    Light to medium hardwood that is easy to work and finishes well.

    Flooring · Interiors

  • 07 · Hardwood

    Red gum

    Deep red hardwood, durable and suited to structural work.

    Stairs · Benchtops · Furniture

  • 08 · Hardwood

    Tasmanian blackwood

    Dark hardwood with a striking grain for high-end joinery.

    Joinery · Furniture · Cladding

  • 09 · Hardwood

    Marri

    Medium hardwood with rich tones and plenty of feature.

    Flooring · Furniture

  • 10 · Hardwood

    Karri

    Strong, durable hardwood with reddish hues.

    Decking · Flooring · Structural

  • 11 · Hardwood

    Kapur

    Dense hardwood for decking and heavy-use applications.

    Decking · Posts · Beams

  • 12 · Hardwood

    Silvertop ash

    Light hardwood with a smooth finish, excellent for joinery.

    Cladding · Joinery · Flooring

  • 13 · Hardwood

    Flooded gum

    Also sold as rose gum. Light timber with natural character.

    Flooring · Joinery

  • 14 · Hardwood

    Brush box

    Heavy hardwood with a fine grain.

    Stair treads · Furniture · Joinery

  • 15 · Hardwood

    Meranti

    Rich reddish-brown tones, from the mahogany family.

    Furniture · Joinery

  • 16 · Softwood

    Cypress pine

    Durable softwood, resistant to termites and decay.

    Posts · Fencing · Cladding

  • 17 · Softwood

    Radiata pine

    Plantation softwood, light and easy to work with.

    Framing · Lining · Interiors
