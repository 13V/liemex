    04 · What we found

    Why the current site is costing you

    We went through liemex.com.au as a customer would. Three things stood out.

    It says it isn't finished

    The product range on the homepage carries the words "Still under construction". A customer deciding who to ring reads that as a reason to ring someone else.

    It disagrees with itself

    The homepage says 40+ years and the About page says over 30. Two different email addresses appear. Small things, but they cost trust.

    Your best asset is buried

    You saw, dress and profile your own timber, and you can match a heritage moulding. That's the reason to choose you over a big-box store, and the current site barely mentions it.

    05 · What we need from you

    Six things to finish it

    None of them take long. The photos would make the biggest difference.

    1. Pick a lookOne of the four above, or the parts of each you like.
    2. Confirm the factsHow many years you've been trading, which email address enquiries should go to, and your Saturday hours (the current site says 10 to 11am).
    3. Better photosMost of the photos on your current site are small. A half-day shoot at the yard, of the boards, the machines and a few finished jobs, would lift the site more than anything else. Sharp photos of each timber would also make the room far more lifelike.
    4. Your logoWe've set the name in type for now. Tell us whether to keep your current logo, tidy it, or go with the new wordmark.
    5. The cataloguesThe three PDF catalogues, so we can host them on the new site.
    6. How deep the product pages goYour current site lists about 356 products one by one. We've gathered them into one clear page per range. If you'd like every product to have its own page, that's a second stage.
